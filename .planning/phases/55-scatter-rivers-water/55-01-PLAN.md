    Extend terrain_vegetation_depth.py with all vegetation deep-dive systems:

    **6.127 — Voronoi clumping:** Generate Voronoi from random seed points. Each cell gets: lean_direction, density_multiplier (0.5-2.0), species_bias. Points in same cell share properties -> natural groves.

    **6.128 — Variable-density Poisson disk:** Dart-throwing where `min_distance = base_dist / density_map(x,z)`. Density from moisture/slope/altitude. Dense forest = 0.5m, sparse alpine = 5m.

    **6.129 — Flow accumulation moisture:** `moisture = normalize(flow_accumulation_from_ridge_map)`. High accumulation = wet valley. Feed as scatter input channel.

    **6.130 — Species compatibility matrix:** `Dict[(biome_a, biome_b), Set[species]]`. At biome boundary, only intersection species spawn. E.g., `("desert","forest") -> {"scrub_oak","dry_grass"}`.

    **6.131 — Corruption zone rings:** 4 concentric rings from epicenter:
    Ring 1 (0-0.2r): bare_twisted_rock + tendrils
    Ring 2 (0.2r-0.4r): dead_trees + bioluminescent_mushrooms
    Ring 3 (0.4r-0.7r): blighted vegetation
    Ring 4 (0.7r-1.0r): normal + occasional blight

    **6.132 — Bioluminescent mushrooms:** emission_color=(0.1,0.4,0.3), emission_strength=0.8. Spawn where damp AND (cave OR corruption ring 2).

    **6.133 — Twisted trunks:** In vegetation_lsystem.py, apply `offset = amp * sin(2*pi*cycles * t / height)` for 2-4 cycles, amp=10-20% of radius. Corruption increases amplitude.

    **6.134 — Altitudinal zonation:** 6 normalized bands: valley_floor(0-0.15), lowland(0.15-0.35), montane(0.35-0.55), subalpine(0.55-0.75), alpine(0.75-0.9), nival(0.9-1.0). Each with species list.

    **6.135 — Forest structure layers:** 4 passes: canopy(15-30m, 0.15-0.25 density), understory(5-15m, density*(1-canopy*0.6)), shrub(1-5m, edge boost), ground(0-1m, moisture-driven).

    **6.136 — Exclusion zones:** List of polygons (buildings/roads/cliffs/water). Point-in-polygon test per scatter point. Density ramps from 0 to 1 over 2-5m from boundary.

    **6.137 — Per-instance variation:** hue_shift=hash%20-10 deg, brightness=0.85+hash*0.30, scale=0.7+hash*0.6, rotation=hash%360. Deterministic via position hash.
